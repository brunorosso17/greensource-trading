Certifications

Claims we can put a certificate behind

Each of these is traceable to a certificate held by the mill or the factory that makes the product. Documents are available to customers on request.

  • FSC® certified wood — FSC® C144358

    Birch from responsibly managed forests, under FSC chain of custody, printed on every box.

  • BRCGS A+ audited facility

    Manufactured in a factory audited to the BRCGS Global Standard for Packaging Materials, at grade A+.

  • Food contact safe

    Tested to United States FDA food-contact requirements, with laboratory reports on file.

  • PFAS free

    No fluorinated treatments, no coatings and no plastic components of any kind.

Before an order is placed we review the factory's current audit, verify that the FSC certificate covers the exact product, and read the food-contact reports in full. Every statement printed on the packaging has to be supported by a document we hold; claims we cannot substantiate do not go on the box.
